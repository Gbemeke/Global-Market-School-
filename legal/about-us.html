About Us

Built to be the school we wished existed.

Global Market School Ltd. was founded to close a real gap: financial markets education that is genuinely rigorous, genuinely honest about risk, and genuinely accessible — regardless of where in the world a student is starting from.

Too much of what's available online falls into one of two traps: content dumbed down to the point of being misleading, or content dressed up to look sophisticated while promising unrealistic, fast returns. We built Global Market School to avoid both.

Our Mission

To provide structured, accurate, and honest education in stocks and forex — from a complete beginner's first lesson through to advanced, institutional-grade concepts — without ever compromising on accuracy for the sake of a sale.

What We Stand For

Accuracy over hype. We do not promise guaranteed profits, because no honest educator can.
