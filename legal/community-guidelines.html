Global Chat

Community Guidelines

Last updated: August 2026

Global Chat is a space for students of Global Market School to encourage each other, ask questions, and share what they're learning. These guidelines exist to keep it that way. By posting in Global Chat, you agree to follow them.

1. Be Respectful

No harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks against other students, instructors, or admins. Disagree with an idea if you like — don't attack the person.

2. This Is Not a Signals Group

Nothing posted by a fellow student in Global Chat — including opinions, chart screenshots, or "calls" — is financial advice from Global Market School, and nobody's personal opinion here is a guaranteed outcome. Do not present your own predictions as certainties, and treat what others post the same way. See our full Risk Disclaimer for how this applies to everything we teach.

3. No Soliciting or Scheming

Do not use Global Chat to solicit money, promote paid signal groups, run giveaways, advertise unrelated businesses, or recruit for schemes of any kind — inside the platform or by redirecting students elsewhere. This includes impersonating staff to ask for funds.

4. Keep It On-Topic

Global Chat is for markets, trading, and the course community. It's not the place for unrelated spam or repeated off-topic posting.

5. No Impersonation

Don't present yourself as a Global Market School instructor, admin, or staff member unless you actually are one.

6. Respect Privacy

Don't post another student's personal or contact information without their consent.

7. Moderation

Admins may remove any post or comment that breaks these guidelines, at their discretion, without prior notice. Repeated or serious violations may lead to losing access to Global Chat. Use the Report option on a post if you believe it breaks these rules — our admins review every report.

A reminder, not a scare tactic Trading carries real risk. Global Chat is peer conversation among students, not professional advice. Before acting on anything you read here — from us or from another student — make your own informed decision, and when in doubt, consult a licensed financial advisor.
