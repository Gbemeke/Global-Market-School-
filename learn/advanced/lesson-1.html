Market Structure: Who You're Really Trading Against

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Every trade you place enters a live order book, matched by price-time priority against market makers, algorithms, and other institutions — not a faceless "the market."
  • Market makers profit from the bid-ask spread and are legally obligated to keep quoting prices, which is what keeps a stock tradable even in thin conditions.
  • Dark pools let large institutions trade blocks privately, which is why big volume can move a stock with no visible order book pressure beforehand.
  • Liquidity — not just price — determines your real execution cost through slippage, especially in less-traded stocks.
