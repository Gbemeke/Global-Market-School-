Regulation, Compliance & Market Integrity

Lesson 5 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Regulators exist to protect market fairness and investor trust, not to eliminate risk — trading risk is yours to manage regardless of regulation.
  • Insider trading and market manipulation are illegal precisely because they break the level playing field every other rule in this course assumes.
  • Circuit breakers and trading halts exist to prevent panic-driven chaos, not to protect you from ordinary losses.
  • A regulated broker and exchange is a baseline requirement, not a detail — it's what makes every other lesson in this course meaningful.
