Risk Management & Position Sizing

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Position size should be calculated from your stop-loss distance and a fixed risk percentage per trade — never a round number of shares.
  • Professionals risk a small, consistent percentage of capital per trade, typically 1-2%, regardless of how confident they feel.
  • A favorable risk-to-reward ratio means you can be wrong more often than right and still be profitable overall.
  • Correlation between your open positions can silently multiply your real risk far beyond what it looks like on paper.
