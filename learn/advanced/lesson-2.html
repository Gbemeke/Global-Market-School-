Reading Financial Statements Like an Analyst

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • The income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement each answer a different question: profitability, financial position, and real cash movement.
  • Net income can be manipulated by accounting choices — cash flow is much harder to fake, which is why professionals weight it heavily.
  • P/E and P/B ratios only mean something in comparison — to the company's own history and to its direct competitors.
  • A cheap-looking valuation without understanding why it's cheap is a value trap, not a bargain.
