Quick Recap

  • Trend direction, defined by a series of higher highs/higher lows or lower highs/lower lows, comes before any indicator.
  • Support and resistance are zones built from past reactions, not exact lines — and they flip roles once broken.
  • Volume confirms or contradicts a price move; a breakout on weak volume is far less trustworthy.
  • No single indicator should ever be traded alone — professionals require confluence across trend, volume, and momentum before acting.
