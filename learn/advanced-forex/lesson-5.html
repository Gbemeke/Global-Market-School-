Building a Professional Trading Plan

Lesson 5 of 5 — Final LessonNarrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A trading plan removes emotional, in-the-moment decisions by defining your rules before you ever open a position.
  • A trading journal turns each trade into data — the fastest way to find and fix your own recurring mistakes.
  • Capital preservation, not maximizing every trade, is the actual primary goal of professional trading.
  • Overtrading — taking low-quality setups out of boredom or impatience — is one of the most common ways discipline quietly breaks down.
