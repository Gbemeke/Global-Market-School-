Macroeconomics & Currency Drivers

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Interest rate differentials are the single biggest macro driver of currency value over the medium term.
  • Inflation erodes currency value; central banks raise rates specifically to fight it, which is why inflation data moves currencies indirectly through rate expectations.
  • GDP growth signals economic health and tends to attract foreign investment, which increases demand for that country's currency.
  • Markets trade expectations, not just data — a "good" number can still cause a currency to fall if it undershoots what was priced in.
