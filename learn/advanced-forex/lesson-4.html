Risk Management in Leveraged Trading

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses equally — it doesn't change your odds, only the size of the swing.
  • A margin call happens when losses erode your account below the broker's required minimum, forcing positions to close automatically.
  • Effective leverage — your total position size relative to your account — matters more than the maximum leverage your broker allows.
  • Professionals treat low, controlled effective leverage as the entire foundation of long-term survival in forex.
