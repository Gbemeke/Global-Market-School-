How the Forex Market Actually Works

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Forex has no central exchange — it's a decentralized network of banks, institutions, and brokers trading directly with each other.
  • The market runs 24 hours across four overlapping sessions, and liquidity shifts dramatically depending on which sessions are active.
  • Central banks and large institutional players, not retail traders, drive the vast majority of real volume and price movement.
  • The London-New York overlap is the highest-liquidity window, and where most serious volatility tends to occur.
