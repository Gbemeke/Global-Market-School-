Advanced Chart Analysis for Forex

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Multi-timeframe analysis — checking the higher timeframe trend before acting on a lower timeframe signal — filters out most false setups.
  • Currency pairs sharing a base or quote currency are often correlated, which can silently double your real exposure.
  • Key forex session opens and closes often produce characteristic volatility patterns worth recognizing.
  • The same technical tools from stocks apply to forex, but must be read alongside session timing and correlation, not in isolation.
