Two Ways to Analyze a Market

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Fundamental analysis studies the business itself; technical analysis studies price behavior and chart patterns.
  • Trend, support/resistance, and volume are the core building blocks of reading a chart.
  • Neither approach is "correct" on its own — most experienced investors use both together.
  • Fundamentals can tell you what to buy; technicals can help with when to buy it.
