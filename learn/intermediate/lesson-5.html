Quick Recap

  • Interest rates set the tone for the entire stock market, not just individual companies.
  • Economic cycles move through expansion and contraction — different sectors perform differently in each phase.
  • Broad market sentiment can lift or drag even fundamentally strong individual stocks.
  • A complete investor reads the company, the chart, and the wider economic environment together.
