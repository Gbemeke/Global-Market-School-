Intermediate Track — Stocks — Lesson 2

Is It Cheap or Expensive?

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Valuation means judging a stock's price relative to what the underlying business actually earns, not the price alone.
  • The P/E ratio only means something compared to the company's own history and its direct competitors.
  • Growth expectations are baked into price — a fast-growing company usually deserves a higher valuation than a stagnant one.
  • Cheap for a clear reason and cheap because the market is wrong are very different situations.
