Reading a Company Like an Investor

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Revenue growth alone doesn't tell you if a company is healthy — profit margins reveal how efficiently it turns sales into profit.
  • A strong balance sheet means manageable debt relative to what the company owns and earns.
  • Consistent, not just high, growth is often the stronger signal of a well-run business.
  • Reading a company means comparing several numbers together, never judging one in isolation.
