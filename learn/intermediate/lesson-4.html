Intermediate Track — Stocks — Lesson 4

Building an Actual Strategy

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A real strategy is written down, specific, and repeatable — not a fresh decision made emotionally every time.
  • Your entry and exit criteria should be defined before you buy, not decided in the moment.
  • A strategy without a defined exit is not actually a strategy.
  • Backtesting your logic against past data helps validate an approach before risking real money on it.
