Options as Strategic Tools

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A call option gives the right to buy at a set price; a put option gives the right to sell at a set price.
  • A covered call generates income from stock you already own by selling upside potential in exchange for a premium.
  • A protective put acts as insurance, capping downside risk on a position you want to keep holding.
  • Options are strategic risk-management tools at this level — not primarily a way to make leveraged directional bets.
