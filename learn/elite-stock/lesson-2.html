Advanced Valuation: Building a DCF Model

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A DCF model values a company based on the cash it's expected to generate in the future, discounted to today's value.
  • The discount rate reflects risk — higher perceived risk means future cash is worth less today.
  • Terminal value captures everything beyond the forecast period and often makes up most of the total valuation.
  • A DCF is only as good as its assumptions — small changes in growth or discount rate can swing the output dramatically.
