Smart Money Concepts: Reading Institutional Order Flow

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • "Smart money" refers to informed institutional capital whose footprints show up in liquidity and order flow, not headlines.
  • Liquidity pools — clusters of resting stop-losses and orders — are frequent institutional targets before a real move.
  • A "stop hunt" or liquidity sweep often precedes genuine institutional accumulation or distribution.
  • Reading order flow is about probability and context, never certainty about what any single institution is doing.
