Portfolio Construction & Correlation at Scale

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Portfolio-level risk depends on how positions move together, not just how risky each one looks alone.
  • Position correlation, sector concentration, and factor exposure all matter beyond simple diversification counts.
  • Rebalancing periodically keeps a portfolio's actual risk aligned with its intended risk.
  • Elite-level portfolio management means managing the whole, not optimizing each position in isolation.
