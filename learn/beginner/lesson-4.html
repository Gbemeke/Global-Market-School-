Risk & Diversifying

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Every investment carries risk — the real question is always how much, and whether you can handle it.
  • Diversifying spreads your money across different companies and sectors so one failure doesn't sink you.
  • Only invest money you won't need soon — panic-selling during a downturn locks in losses that would otherwise recover.
  • Risk tolerance is personal; there's no single "correct" amount of risk for everyone.
