Quick Recap

  • A stock market is simply an organized place where ownership shares of companies are bought and sold.
  • When you buy a share, you genuinely own a tiny slice of that real company, not a bet on it.
  • Stock prices move because buyers and sellers constantly disagree on what a company is worth right now.
  • An index like the S&P 500 tracks a basket of companies together, giving a snapshot of the broader market.
