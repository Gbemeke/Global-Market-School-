Intermediate Track — Forex — Lesson 2

Is a Currency Cheap or Expensive?

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Interest rate differentials between two countries are the clearest signal of relative currency value.
  • Purchasing power parity compares what money actually buys in different countries as a long-term value anchor.
  • Currencies can stay "mispriced" relative to fundamentals for a long time — timing still matters.
  • Relative value, not an absolute number, is the entire premise of every currency pair.
