Two Ways to Analyze Forex

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Fundamental analysis in forex studies the economies behind each currency; technical analysis studies the chart itself.
  • Trend, support/resistance, and volume apply to forex charts the same way they apply to stock charts.
  • Fundamentals often explain the larger, longer-term direction; technicals often help refine entry timing.
  • Combining both gives a fuller, more reliable picture than relying on either alone.
