Reading a Currency Like a Trader

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A currency's real strength reflects its economy's underlying health, not just its exchange rate on any given day.
  • GDP growth, employment, and trade balance are core economic health indicators traders track.
  • Central bank policy direction matters as much as the current interest rate level itself.
  • Reading a currency means reading an entire economy, not a single number.
