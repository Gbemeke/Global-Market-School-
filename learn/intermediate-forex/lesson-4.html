Intermediate Track — Forex — Lesson 4

Building an Actual Forex Strategy

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A forex strategy needs written entry criteria combining fundamental bias and technical confirmation.
  • Exit criteria — both profit target and stop-loss — must be defined before entering, not decided mid-trade.
  • Position sizing must account for leverage, not just the dollar amount you're depositing.
  • A trading journal turns your forex results into real data you can actually learn from.
