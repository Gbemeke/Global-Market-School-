Institutional Execution & Algorithmic Awareness

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Algorithms account for the majority of daily forex volume, reacting to price in milliseconds.
  • Round numbers and obvious technical levels attract algorithmic activity, creating predictable short-term behavior.
  • Execution algorithms used by large institutions are designed to minimize their own market impact.
  • Understanding algorithmic behavior helps you avoid being on the wrong side of predictable, mechanical flows.
