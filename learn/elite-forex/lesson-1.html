Central Bank Policy Analysis in Practice

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Reading a central bank statement's exact wording matters as much as the rate decision itself.
  • "Dovish" and "hawkish" describe the tone and direction of a central bank's language, not just its actions.
  • Forward guidance shapes market expectations well before any policy change actually happens.
  • Comparing policy divergence between two central banks is the real trade, not one bank's decision alone.
