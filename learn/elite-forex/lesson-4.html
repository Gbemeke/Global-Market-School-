Elite Group — Forex — Lesson 4

Macro Portfolio Construction Across Currencies

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Multiple currency positions should be viewed as one combined book of currency exposure, not separate trades.
  • Net exposure to any single currency across all your pairs matters more than any one pair alone.
  • A themed macro view, expressed across several correlated pairs, is a deliberate professional technique — not accidental overexposure.
  • Elite-level forex risk management means managing total currency exposure, the same discipline as stock portfolio construction.
