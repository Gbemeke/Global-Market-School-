Cross-Asset Correlation

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Bond yields and currency values are closely linked through the same interest rate expectations.
  • Commodity prices move certain currencies through trade revenue, independent of that country's own data.
  • Equity market sentiment often spills into forex through broad risk-on/risk-off flows.
  • Elite-level forex analysis means watching bonds, commodities, and equities together, not forex in isolation.
