Advanced Risk Control During Live Trading

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A daily loss limit, decided in advance, protects an account from a single bad session spiraling further.
  • Trailing stops let a live position capture more of a strong move while still protecting realized gains.
  • Reducing position size after consecutive losses is a deliberate, disciplined response, not a sign of weakness.
  • Advanced risk control means having a real, pre-decided plan for exactly when to stop trading entirely.
