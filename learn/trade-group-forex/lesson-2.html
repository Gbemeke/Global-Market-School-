Executing Around High-Impact News Events

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Spreads widen and slippage increases sharply in the seconds around high-impact scheduled news.
  • Standing in a live position through a major release is a deliberate choice, not a default — plan for it or flatten before it.
  • Trading the reaction after the initial spike often offers cleaner conditions than trading the spike itself.
  • A written news-event plan removes guesswork exactly when conditions are least forgiving of hesitation.
