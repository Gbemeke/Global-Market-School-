Managing Multiple Concurrent Forex Positions

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Live forex positions must be checked for currency correlation continuously, not just at the moment each is opened.
  • Total account leverage across all open positions matters more in fast-moving live conditions than in calm analysis.
  • A live dashboard view of net currency exposure prevents accidental overexposure during an active session.
  • Managing multiple live forex positions well is a direct, practical application of the Elite Forex portfolio lesson.
