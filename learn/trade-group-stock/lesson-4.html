Managing a Live Multi-Position Portfolio

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Combined open risk across all live positions matters more than any single position's risk in isolation.
  • A simple running log of open positions and total risk prevents losing track during an active session.
  • New positions should be evaluated against your remaining risk budget, not just their own individual merit.
  • Knowing when to stop opening new positions for the session is itself a real, deliberate skill.
