Reading Live Order Flow Like the CEO

Quick Recap

  • Live order flow reading means watching real-time buying and selling pressure, not just the completed chart.
  • Time and sales data shows the actual pace and size of trades happening right now, second by second.
  • Absorption — heavy selling that fails to push price down — often signals real buying strength underneath.
  • Trade Group's live approach builds directly on the Elite-level order flow concepts, applied in real time.
