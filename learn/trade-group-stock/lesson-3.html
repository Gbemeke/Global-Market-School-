Scaling In and Out of Positions

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Scaling in means entering a position in stages rather than all at once, reducing the cost of a slightly early entry.
  • Scaling out means taking partial profit at multiple targets, balancing locked-in gains with further upside.
  • Scaling requires the same total-position-size discipline as a single entry — it's not a way around risk management.
  • This technique works best on positions with real conviction, not as a substitute for a clear thesis.
