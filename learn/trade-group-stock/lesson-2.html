Trade Execution Under Real-Time Pressure

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Pre-deciding your entry, stop, and target before the market opens removes pressure from the actual moment of execution.
  • Hesitation past your planned entry point often costs more than a slightly imperfect but timely entry.
  • "Waiting for CEO confirmation" exists specifically to slow down impulsive, pressure-driven decisions.
  • Real-time execution discipline is a trainable skill, built through repeated, deliberate live practice.
