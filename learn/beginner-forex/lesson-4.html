Leverage & Margin

Lesson 4 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Leverage lets you control a larger position than your account balance alone would allow.
  • Margin is the portion of your own money set aside as a good-faith deposit for a leveraged position.
  • Leverage magnifies both gains and losses by the same amount — it's a multiplier, not an edge.
  • Beginners should use low leverage and small position sizes while they're still learning.
