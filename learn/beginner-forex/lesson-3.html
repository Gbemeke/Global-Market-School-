Beginner Track — Forex — Lesson 3

Major Currency Pairs & What Moves Them

Lesson 3 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • The "majors" are the most heavily traded pairs, all involving the US dollar, and offer the tightest spreads.
  • Interest rates, economic data, and overall risk sentiment are the main forces behind currency moves.
  • "Risk-on" and "risk-off" describe whether traders broadly favor riskier or safer currencies at a given time.
  • Starting with major pairs as a beginner means better liquidity and more predictable trading conditions.
