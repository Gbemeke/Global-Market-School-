How a Forex Trade Actually Works

Lesson 2 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • A pip is the smallest standard price movement in a currency pair, usually the fourth decimal place.
  • A lot is a standardized trade size — standard, mini, and micro lots let you control how much you're risking.
  • Going "long" means buying the first currency; going "short" means selling it.
  • Your profit or loss is the pip movement multiplied by your position size.
