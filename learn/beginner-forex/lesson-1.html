What Is Forex?

Lesson 1 of 5Narrated presentation — 5 slides

Quick Recap

  • Forex is the global market where one currency is exchanged for another.
  • Currencies always trade in pairs — you're always buying one currency while selling another.
  • It's the largest, most liquid financial market in the world, trading nearly 24 hours a day on weekdays.
  • You've likely already participated in forex informally, every time you've exchanged money while traveling.
